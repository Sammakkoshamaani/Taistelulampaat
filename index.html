    How to play

    1. The pasture is built from 4 tiles of 4 fields per player.
    2. Setup: in turn, each player puts their whole flock of 16 sheep on an empty field at the edge of the pasture.
    3. Your turn: pick one of your stacks with 2+ sheep and split it – leave at least one sheep behind and move the rest.
    4. The moving sheep go in a straight line (6 directions) and must go as far as possible, until they hit the edge or an occupied field.
    5. If you can't move, you're skipped. The game ends when nobody can move.
    6. Winner: the player occupying the most fields. Ties are broken by the largest connected group of fields.

    Tip: big stacks in open areas are flexible – a stack trapped with many sheep is wasted!
